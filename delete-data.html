Delete your account and data

Buzzsaw Brigade keeps your account and progress on your device only. We do not hold a copy. Here is how to remove it.

Delete the account in the app

  1. Open Buzzsaw Brigade and tap the settings button.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This erases the display name, email address and password from your device. Your game progress stays.

Delete all game data

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps, then Buzzsaw Brigade.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage.

This erases your progress, backups, settings and account from the device. Uninstalling the app does the same. Deleted data cannot be recovered unless you saved a save code.

Ask us to delete data

If you no longer have the app, or you emailed us and want those messages deleted, send a request. You do not need to reinstall the game.

Email pcfixer@helpdeskmastercomputersupport.org with the subject "Buzzsaw Brigade data deletion request".

Include the email address you used in the game or to contact us. We confirm by reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the in-game account details, your progress and backups (on your device), and any support emails you ask us to remove.
  • Kept: nothing. We do not store game data, so there is nothing left on our side once your device data and emails are gone.
