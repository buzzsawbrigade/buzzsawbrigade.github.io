The game

Build a squad. Hold the line.

Buzzsaw Brigade is a summon and defend strategy game. You place troopers beside a winding road, and the Scrapborn march down it. Each trooper has a weapon style, a rank and a set of gear you can upgrade between matches. The game is free to play and plays fully offline.

17 troopers

Five weapon styles across Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic ranks.

20 battlefields

Sixteen painted maps and four dungeons, each with its own road for every difficulty.

49 enemies

Thirty eight Scrapborn types and eleven bosses with their own attacks.

Plain and simple

No ads. No tracking.

The game keeps your progress on your phone. It has no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and it does not send your data to anyone. You can play without an account.

Summon and crate odds are listed on the summon odds page and inside the game before every draw.
