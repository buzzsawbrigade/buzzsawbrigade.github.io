Common questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. Every mode works without an account. The optional account only stores a name and email on your phone so the game can greet you and keep a profile.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. The game saves on its own after matches and purchases with in-game currency. Open Settings, then Save Data, to make a backup or copy a save code.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On the old phone open Settings, then Save Data, then Export Save Code, and copy the code somewhere safe. On the new phone use Import Save Code and paste it in.

I lost my progress. Can you get it back?

Your save never reaches us, so we cannot restore it from our side. Check Settings, then Save Data, for a backup made on your phone. If you saved a save code, import it.

How do I delete my account or my data?

See the delete your data page. You can delete the account inside the game, or clear the app storage to erase everything.

Does the game cost money or show ads?

The game is free and has no ads. The shop uses in-game currency that you earn by playing. If real money purchases are added later, the store listing and this site will say so first.

What are the summon odds?

They are listed on the summon odds page, and the game shows the exact odds for your next draw before you spend anything.

The game runs slowly on my phone.

Open Settings and turn on Reduce Effects. It cuts particles and sounds for older phones. If it is still slow, send us your phone model.

Is there online play?

Not in the current version. Online co-op is planned. When it arrives, the privacy policy will be updated first and the game will explain what it sends.
