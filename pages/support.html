Common questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. Every mode works without an account. The optional account only stores a name and email on your device so the game can greet you and keep a profile. Online co-op uses an anonymous online account that the game makes for you, with no email or password.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only, whether you play on a phone or on a Windows PC. The game saves on its own after matches and purchases with in-game currency. Open Settings, then Save Data, to make a backup or copy a save code.

How do I move my progress to a new phone or PC?

On the old device open Settings, then Save Data, then Export Save Code, and copy the code somewhere safe. On the new device use Import Save Code and paste it in.

I lost my progress. Can you get it back?

Your save never reaches us, so we cannot restore it from our side. Check Settings, then Save Data, for a backup made on your device. If you saved a save code, import it.

How do I delete my account or my data?

See the delete your data page. You can delete the device account and the online co-op account inside the game, or clear the game's storage to erase everything on the device.

Does the game cost money or show ads?

The game is free and has no ads. The shop uses in-game currency that you earn by playing. If real money purchases are added later, the store listings and this site will say so first.

What are the summon odds?

They are listed on the summon odds page, and the game shows the exact odds for your next draw before you spend anything.

The game runs slowly on my device.

Open Settings and turn on Reduce Effects. It cuts particles and sounds for older devices. If it is still slow, send us your phone model or PC details.

Is there online play?

Yes. Online co-op for two players is optional. You can join a quick match with another player or make a room and send the code to a friend. It needs an internet connection. Solo play works offline. The privacy policy lists what online co-op stores.

Online co-op will not connect.

Check your internet connection and make sure the game is up to date, because old versions cannot join. If the online buttons are missing, the co-op server is switched off for maintenance. Try again later, and email us if it lasts more than a day.

Are the guild members and the chat real players?

Not in this version. The guild members, the friends list and the chat are simulated on your device. The only real player you meet is your partner in online co-op.
