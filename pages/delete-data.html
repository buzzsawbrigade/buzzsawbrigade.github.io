Delete your account and data

Buzzsaw Brigade keeps your progress on your device. If you played online co-op, our co-op server also holds a small anonymous online account. Here is how to remove each one.

Delete your online co-op account

You only have one if you opened an online mode. In the game:

  1. Open Buzzsaw Brigade and open Settings.
  2. In the Online Play section, choose Delete Online Account.
  3. Confirm.

The server deletes your online player record and all of its match records at once. Your game on your device is not affected. Do this before you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Without the game: in Settings, Online Play, choose Copy Deletion Details, then paste them into the delete account page on the co-op server. If you no longer have the game installed, email us as described below.

Delete the device account in the game

  1. Open Buzzsaw Brigade and open Settings.
  2. In the Account section, choose Delete Account.
  3. Choose Delete to confirm.

This erases the display name, email address and password from your device. Your game progress stays.

Delete all game data on the device

On Android:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps, then Buzzsaw Brigade.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage.

On Windows (Microsoft Store version):

  1. Open Windows Settings.
  2. Choose Apps, then Installed apps, and find Buzzsaw Brigade.
  3. Open the menu beside it and choose Advanced options, then Reset. Or choose Uninstall.

This erases your progress, backups, settings and device account from the device. Uninstalling the game does the same. Deleted data cannot be recovered unless you saved a save code.

Ask us to delete data

If you no longer have the app, or you emailed us and want those messages deleted, send a request. You do not need to reinstall the game.

Email {{EMAIL}} with the subject "Buzzsaw Brigade data deletion request".

To delete an online co-op account, include your online player ID if you have it (it starts with BB and is shown in Settings under Online Play) or the display name you used and roughly when you last played. To delete emails, include the address you wrote from. We confirm by reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your online co-op account and its match records (on our server), the device account details, your progress and backups (on your device), and any support emails you ask us to remove.
  • Kept: nothing. Once your online account, your device data and your emails are gone, there is nothing left on our side.
