Summon and crate odds

These are the base odds the game uses. The summon and crate screens show the exact odds for your next draw, with your Luck Rating and pity counter applied, before you spend anything.

Gems and other currencies in Buzzsaw Brigade are earned by playing. This version has no real money purchases. If purchases are added later, these odds will still be shown before every draw.

Unit Summon

Each draw gives one trooper. The first copy unlocks the trooper, and later copies become Unit Shards.

RankBase chance per draw
Mythic0.6%
Legendary3%
Epic18%
Rare78.4%
  • From the 61st draw without a Legendary or Mythic, the Legendary chance rises by 6 points each draw.
  • The 80th draw without a Legendary or Mythic is always Legendary or better: 85% Legendary and 15% Mythic.
  • The featured trooper of the day gets half of all results of its rank.
  • Your Luck Rating raises the Mythic, Legendary and Epic chances. The caps are 10% Mythic, 20% Legendary and 34% Epic.

Crates

Each crate open gives one item. Epic or better results can be a trooper instead of gear: 40% for Epic, 40% for Legendary and 25% for Mythic.
