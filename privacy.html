Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026. This policy covers the Buzzsaw Brigade game on Android and on Windows (Microsoft Store), and every other version of the game.

Summary

Buzzsaw Brigade saves your game on your device. Solo play sends none of your game data to us. If you choose to play online co-op, the game makes an anonymous online account on our co-op server so it can match you with a partner and run the match. The game has no ads, no analytics and no third party trackers, and you can delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Buzzsaw Brigade is made and published by the Buzzsaw Brigade team, an independent developer in the United States. In this policy "we" and "us" mean the Buzzsaw Brigade team. You can reach us at pcfixer@helpdeskmastercomputersupport.org.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in its own private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us.

DataWhy the game keeps it
Game progress: troopers, gear, upgrades, currencies, levels, quests and match resultsSo you can continue where you left off
Settings: sound, volume and gameplay optionsSo your choices stay the same next time
A random player ID made on your deviceShown in Settings so you can tell saves apart. It is not linked to your device hardware.
Backups of your saveSo you can restore progress if something goes wrong
Optional account: the display name, email address and password you type inOnly if you choose to create an account. It is used to sign in on this device.
Guild, friends list and chatIn this version the guild members, the friends list and the chat are simulated on your device. Messages you type in chat are not sent to other players or to us.

The optional account never leaves your device. We never see the email or password. The password is kept in the game's private storage and is not encrypted, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else. You can play every mode without an account.

If you use Export Save Code, the game shows your save as a code on screen. That code only goes where you choose to paste it.

Online co-op (optional)

Online co-op lets two players defend the same map together. It is optional. The first time you open an online mode (a quick match or a room with a friend), the game creates an anonymous online account for you on our co-op server. There is no email, password or sign in for it.

What the co-op server storesWhyKept

A random device ID made by the game. It is not a hardware ID or an advertising ID.To find the same online account again on the same deviceUntil you delete your online account

The game also keeps a sign in token for the online account in its private storage on your device, so you stay the same online player next time.

Tester access: if we give you free tester access, we store the email address or player ID you gave us for that purpose, with a short note. The game only downloads a scrambled, one way form of that list and checks it on your device.

Who sees your data

  • Your co-op partner sees your display name during a match. Pick a name that does not identify you.
  • The co-op server runs on Cloudflare, which processes the data for us as our hosting provider. Cloudflare may log technical details of requests, such as IP addresses, for security and operations under its own privacy policy. Our own server records do not store your IP address. Cloudflare operates in many countries, so data may be processed outside the country where you live.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else. We would only disclose it if the law required us to.

What we do not collect

  • Solo play sends none of your game data off your device. Only optional online co-op stores data on our server, as described above. There is no cloud save in this version.
  • We do not collect your real name, location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or the advertising ID.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash reporting services, and no third party code in the game collects data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

This version of the game has no real money purchases. If you get the game from Google Play or the Microsoft Store, the download is handled by Google or Microsoft under their own privacy policies.

Permissions and internet use

The Android app asks for one permission, internet access. The Windows version uses your internet connection in the same way and asks for no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

The internet connection is used for three things: online co-op, opening web pages such as this one when you tap a link in Settings, and a short check when the game starts and every so often while it is open. In that check the game asks the co-op server whether online play is switched on and downloads the scrambled tester list. The check carries no player ID, name or save data. If you are offline the game skips it and solo play works as normal.

How your data is protected

Your save is kept in storage that belongs to the game, under the protections Android and Windows give every app. Everything the game sends to the co-op server travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). Access to the server records is limited to the developer. This site is served over HTTPS.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Online co-op data stays on the server until you delete your online account. You can:

  • Delete your online account in the game: Settings, then Online Play, then Delete Online Account. This removes your online account and all of its match records from the server at once. Your game on your device is not affected.
  • Delete your online account without the game: use the delete account page on the co-op server, or email us.
  • Delete the optional device account in the game: Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. Your progress stays.
  • Erase everything on the device: clear the game's storage or uninstall it. Do this after deleting the online account if you have one.

Full steps for Android and Windows are on the delete your data page. If you email us, we keep the message only as long as needed to answer it, and we delete it on request.

Children

Buzzsaw Brigade has cartoon action and is meant for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Online co-op does not ask for a real name, email address or age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Online co-op is your choice. If you never open an online mode, no online account is made. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. To ask for a copy of your online co-op data, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to us with your online player ID (shown in Settings under Online Play). We answer within 30 days. You can change your display name in the game at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version collects or sends anything new, such as cloud saves, online leaderboards, ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the store listings before that version is released. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
