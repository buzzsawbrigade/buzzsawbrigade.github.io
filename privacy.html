Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026. This policy covers the Buzzsaw Brigade app for Android and every other version of the game.

Summary

Buzzsaw Brigade does not collect, send or share your personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no third party trackers. Everything the game saves stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

Who we are

Buzzsaw Brigade is made and published by the Buzzsaw Brigade team, an independent developer in the United States. In this policy "we" and "us" mean the Buzzsaw Brigade team. You can reach us at pcfixer@helpdeskmastercomputersupport.org.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in the app's private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

DataWhy the game keeps it
Game progress: troopers, gear, upgrades, currencies, levels, quests and match resultsSo you can continue where you left off
Settings: sound, volume and gameplay optionsSo your choices stay the same next time
A random player ID made on your deviceShown in Settings so you can tell saves apart. It is not linked to you or your device hardware.
Backups of your saveSo you can restore progress if something goes wrong
Optional account: the display name, email address and password you type inOnly if you choose to create an account. It is used to sign in on this device.

The optional account never leaves your device. We never see the name, email or password. The password is kept in the app's private storage and is not encrypted, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else. You can play every mode without an account.

If you use Export Save Code, the game shows your save as a code on screen. That code only goes where you choose to paste it.

What we do not collect

  • We do not send any game data off your device. There is no server, cloud save or account system run by us in this version.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or device identifiers such as the advertising ID.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash reporting services, and no third party code in the app collects data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Buying the app or anything else through Google Play is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. This version of the game has no real money purchases.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission, internet access. It is used to open web pages such as this one when you tap a link in Settings, and it is reserved for online co-op in a future version. The current version does not send your game data over the internet. The app does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

How your data is protected

Your save is kept in storage that only the Buzzsaw Brigade app can read, under the protections Android gives every app. Because we do not receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side that could be exposed. Any web page we run, including this site, is served over HTTPS.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your device until you delete it. We hold no copy, so we keep nothing after you delete it. You can:

  • Delete the optional account in the game: Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. Your progress stays.
  • Erase everything: in Android settings open Apps, then Buzzsaw Brigade, then Storage, then Clear storage. Uninstalling the app also erases it.

Full steps are on the delete your data page. If you email us, we keep the message only as long as needed to answer it, and we delete it on request.

Children

Buzzsaw Brigade has cartoon action and is meant for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, because the game does not collect personal data at all. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data from the app. If you have emailed us and want that message deleted or a copy of it, write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that collects or sends data, such as online co-op, cloud saves, ads or purchases, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

Contact
